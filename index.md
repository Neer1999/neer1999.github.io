## 🎯 What Is This?

**whoknows-android-trivia** is a fun, fast-paced trivia game for your Android phone or tablet. It's like a pub quiz in your pocket! You get questions from all sorts of categories—movies, science, history, sports, and more—and you have to answer them before time runs out. The more you answer correctly, the higher your score. It's simple, addictive, and perfect for passing time or challenging your friends.

The app is designed to look and feel like a classic arcade game. Bright colors, big buttons, and satisfying animations make every round exciting. Whether you have a minute or an hour, you can jump in and play.

- **🎚️ Three Difficulty Levels**: Start with easy questions and work your way up to expert-level brain-teasers.

## 🧰 Behind the Scenes (For the Curious)

If you're interested in how the app is built, here's a little technical peek. This app is developed using modern Android technologies:

- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI Toolkit**: Jetpack Compose with Material 3 design
- **Architecture**: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
- **Networking**: Retrofit
- **Database**: Room
- **Asynchronous Operations**: Coroutines
- **Data Source**: Open Trivia DB (opentdb.com)

This stack ensures the app is fast, reliable, and easy to maintain. But you don't need to understand any of this to enjoy the game!
